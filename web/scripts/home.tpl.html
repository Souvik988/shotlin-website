Frequently asked

Good questions.

Still curious? Message us on WhatsApp — a founder replies.

How long does it take to deliver a launch kit?

A launch kit starts from a product we've already shipped, so most of the time goes into your branding, content, integrations and store review. You get a written timeline after the first call.

Do you hand over the complete source code?

Yes. You own the code, the accounts and the servers — we deploy on infrastructure in your name.

Launch kit or custom build — what's the difference?

A launch kit is a proven product adapted to your brand: faster and more affordable. A custom build is designed and engineered from scratch around a unique idea.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Yes. We monitor, update and keep improving your product, deployed through Opslin on servers you own.

Which technologies do you use?

Flutter for Android & iOS; Next.js, React and Node.js for web; Go and Docker for infrastructure; RAG, MCP and LLM tooling for AI.
