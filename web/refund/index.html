Refund policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Milestone-based work

Projects are billed per milestone. If you cancel before a milestone's work has started, the payment for that milestone is refunded in full.

Work in progress

If you cancel during a milestone, we refund the portion of that milestone not yet worked on and hand over everything completed so far.

Completed milestones

Milestones that have been delivered and approved are non-refundable, and the deliverables are yours to keep.

Launch kits

Setup and branding fees follow the same milestone rules. Third-party costs such as app store accounts, domains and servers are paid directly by you and are not refundable by us.

How to request a refund

Email hello@shotlin.in with your project name. We reply within five working days.
