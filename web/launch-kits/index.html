Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Still curious? Message us on WhatsApp — a founder replies.

How long does it take to deliver a launch kit?

Most of the work is branding, content, integrations and store review, because the product itself is already built. You get a written timeline after the first call.

Do you provide the complete source code?

Yes. You own the code, the app store listings, the accounts and the servers.

Can I add features to a launch kit?

Yes. Kits are a starting point — we add the features, integrations and workflows your business needs.

Do you provide maintenance and support after delivery?

Yes. We monitor, update and improve the product after launch, deployed through Opslin on servers you own.

What technologies do the kits use?

Flutter for Android and iOS, Next.js and Node.js for dashboards and APIs, PostgreSQL, and Docker-based deployment.
