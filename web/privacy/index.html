Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us: your name, contact details and project information when you message us on WhatsApp, email us or book a call.

The project brief form

The brief form on this site does not store anything on our servers. It opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

How we use it

To reply to you, prepare proposals and deliver projects. We never sell your information or add you to marketing lists without asking.

Cookies & analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in the future, this page will say so.

Third parties

Messages you send through WhatsApp or email are also handled by those providers under their own privacy policies.
