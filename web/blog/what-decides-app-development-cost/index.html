Key takeaways

  • Cost is driven mostly by how many apps and user roles you need.
  • Every integration — payments, maps, WhatsApp, GST — adds work.
  • Real-time features need extra engineering and testing.
  • Budget for stores, servers and maintenance, not just the build.

"How much does an app cost?" is the most common question we get, and the honest answer is: it depends on a handful of decisions you can actually control.

1. How many apps you really need

A delivery business is rarely one app. It's a customer app, a vendor or store app, a rider app and an admin dashboard. Each one adds screens, roles and testing.

2. Integrations

Payments, maps, WhatsApp, SMS, invoicing and GST, ERP or POS hardware — every integration adds work, and some add ongoing fees.

3. Real-time features

Live tracking, chat, auctions and dashboards that update instantly need more careful engineering than screens that just load data.

4. The parts nobody budgets for

  • App store accounts, review cycles and compliance.
  • Servers, monitoring, backups and SSL.
  • Maintenance as Android, iOS and libraries change.
